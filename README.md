## What it is

A cozy budget planner for your phone and your computer. Your purse shows what's
left until payday and a share for today that shrinks as you spend. Every bit you
save colors in a little square of a picture: the Eiffel Tower for a Paris trip,
an umbrella for a safety cushion. There are savings challenges you roll a die
or scratch a square for, a payday ritual, a cute bills calendar, no-spend
stickers and three looks to pick from.

The demo is the real app, opened on an example purse so there is something to
explore straight away. You can log up to 40 spends of your own before it asks
you to buy, and save a backup that the full app opens with everything in place.

## Your data stays yours

Everything you type is held in your own browser and goes nowhere else: no
server, no account, no tracking.

The full app adds what needs its own private server: your phone and computer
kept in step, Apple Pay taps that log themselves on iPhone, and reminders sent
to your phone. No subscription, pay once.
